<dash>
name: $addUserReactions
desc: Puts emoji reactions on the message that triggered your command.
category: Components

<block type="text" title="How does the command work?">
text: $addUserReactions sticks your chosen emojis onto the message the member just sent, so the reaction shows up right under what they wrote.
text: List as many as you like, separated by semicolons, and anything the bot can work out inside them is filled in first, which means saved values work as emoji too.
text: Each emoji goes on one at a time with a short pause in between, and you can have up to 20 of them in a single call.
</block>

<block type="list" title="How to use it in the app">
item: Open the Commands tab
item: Tap + to start a new command
item: Give it a name in the Name field
item: Set the Prefix field to !react
item: Paste the code below into the editor
item: Tap Save
</block>

<block type="code" title="Syntax">
text: $addUserReactions[emoji1; emoji2; emoji3; ...]
</block>

<block type="code" title="Ready-to-copy Example">
text: $addUserReactions[👍; ❤️]
</block>

<block type="callout" kind="note">
text: If Discord is busy and answers with a rate limit, the bot waits and gives that one emoji a second try before carrying on with the rest.
</block>

<block type="callout" kind="warning">
text: Twenty emojis is the ceiling, and anything beyond that is refused with an error.
text: If your text holds no emojis at all the command simply does nothing and says nothing, and the bot needs Add Reactions in that channel.
</block>
</dash>

<details>
syntax: $addUserReactions[emoji1; emoji2; emoji3; ...]
param: emoji|text|Required|One or more emoji to react with, separated by semicolons. Values inside them are filled in first, then every emoji found is added in order.
</details>
