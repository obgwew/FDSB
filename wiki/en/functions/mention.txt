<dash>
name: $mention
desc: Gives you a clickable tag for the member who ran the command.
category: Info

<block type="text" title="How does the command work?">
text: $mention hands you the tag of the member whose message triggered your command. Put it inside your own line of text and the bot posts a name that lights up in the channel and pings that person. Nothing else is needed to make it work, which makes it the shortest way to greet whoever is standing in front of you right now.
</block>

<block type="list" title="How to use it in the app">
item: Open the Commands tab
item: Tap + to start a new command
item: Give it a name in the Name field
item: Set the Prefix field to !greet
item: Write your script in the editor
item: Paste the line below wherever you need it
item: Tap Save
</block>

<block type="code" title="Syntax">
text: $mention
</block>

<block type="code" title="Ready-to-copy Example">
text: Hello $mention!
</block>

<block type="callout" kind="note">
text: What gets sent is the real Discord tag, which looks like this in your script: <@123456789>. In the channel it shows up as the member's name, clicking it opens their profile, and the person also gets a notification.
</block>
</dash>
