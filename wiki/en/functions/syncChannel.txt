<dash>
name: $syncChannel
desc: Synchronizes a channel's permissions with its category.
category: Channel Management

<block type="text" title="How does the command work?">
text: With no argument, $syncChannel targets the channel where the script was triggered. With one argument, it targets the channel identified by the numeric ID. The channel must be a regular channel that belongs to a category. If permissions are already synchronized, the command logs a no-op.
</block>

<block type="code" title="Syntax">
text: $syncChannel[(channel ID or mention)]
</block>

<block type="code" title="Ready-to-copy Example">
text: $syncChannel[123456789012345678]
</block>

<block type="callout" kind="warning">
text: The command must run in a server, and the bot needs permission to manage channel and role permissions. Categories cannot be synchronized directly.
</block>

<block type="callout" kind="note">
text: In the current interpreter this is a standalone command. Its inline resolver returns an empty value and does not perform the synchronization.
</block>
</dash>

<details>
syntax: $syncChannel[(channel ID or mention)]
param: channelID|snowflake|Optional|The channel to synchronize; defaults to the current channel
</details>
