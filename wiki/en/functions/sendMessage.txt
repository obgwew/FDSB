<dash>
name: $sendMessage
desc: Posts a line of text in the channel where the command was used.
category: Messaging

<block type="text" title="How does the command work?">
text: $sendMessage puts whatever you write in the brackets into the channel, and any values or placeholders inside it are filled in first. It takes a single piece of text, and that is the line members see appear. Buttons you have lined up with $addButton ride along on the same message.
</block>

<block type="list" title="How to use it in the app">
item: Open the Commands tab
item: Tap + to start a new command
item: Give it a name in the Name field
item: Set the Prefix field to !sendMessage
item: Write your script in the editor
item: Paste the line below wherever you need it
item: Tap Save
</block>

<block type="code" title="Syntax">
text: $sendMessage[text]
</block>

<block type="code" title="Ready-to-copy Example">
text: $sendMessage[Hello, $authorName!]
</block>

<block type="callout" kind="note">
text: One piece of text only: a semicolon inside your message splits it in two and the bot stops with an error, so leave them out. Discord formatting such as **bold**, italics and links comes through as usual.
</block>
</dash>

<details>
syntax: $sendMessage[text]
param: text|text|Required|The words to post. Values inside them are filled in first.
</details>
