<dash>
name: $suppressErrors
desc: Hides the bot's error messages and shows one message of your own instead.
category: Access Control

<block type="text" title="How does the command work?">
text: $suppressErrors tells the bot to keep its mistakes to itself for the rest of the command. Once the line is anywhere in your script, a syntax slip, a bad value or a failed action no longer prints its usual explanation in the channel. Put a short message in the brackets and that is what members see instead, or leave it empty and the failure passes by in silence.
</block>

<block type="list" title="How to use it in the app">
item: Open the Commands tab
item: Tap + to start a new command
item: Give it a name in the Name field
item: Set the Prefix field to !welcome
item: Write your script in the editor
item: Paste the line below wherever you need it
item: Tap Save
</block>

<block type="code" title="Syntax">
text: $suppressErrors[custom message]
</block>

<block type="code" title="Ready-to-copy Example">
text: $suppressErrors[Something went wrong! Please try again.]
</block>

<block type="callout" kind="note">
text: The bot spots this line before your command even starts, so it can sit anywhere and does not have to come first. Whatever went wrong is still recorded on the bot's side, it simply no longer reaches the channel.
</block>

<block type="callout" kind="warning">
text: Lovely when you want a tidy channel, but it makes genuine problems much harder to track down. The command still stops where it failed, it just stops quietly.
</block>
</dash>

<details>
syntax: $suppressErrors[(custom message)]
param: custom message|text|Optional|What to send to the channel when anything goes wrong. Leave it out and nothing is shown at all.
</details>
