<dash>
name: $month
desc: Gives the current month number, always two digits, from 01 to 12.
category: Timing & Delays

<block type="text" title="How does the command work?">
text: $month reads the clock and hands back the month we are in, always two digits wide, so January reads 01 and December reads 12. Drop it into a line of text and the digits land where you put them, or leave it on a line of its own and the bot posts the figure in the channel. Sit it between $day and $year to build a date of your own.
</block>

<block type="list" title="How to use it in the app">
item: Open the Commands tab
item: Tap + to start a new command
item: Give it a name in the Name field
item: Set the Prefix field to !month
item: Write your script in the editor
item: Paste the line below wherever you need it
item: Tap Save
</block>

<block type="code" title="Syntax">
text: $month
</block>

<block type="code" title="Ready-to-copy Example">
text: $sendMessage[Today is $day/$month/$year]
</block>

<block type="callout" kind="note">
text: The number runs from 01 to 12 and is always two digits wide, so $day/$month/$year lines up as something like 17/10/2026.
</block>

<block type="callout" kind="important">
text: $day, $month and $year all read the bot's own local clock. The timezone set with $time is followed only by $hour, $minute and $second.
</block>
</dash>
