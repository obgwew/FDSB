<dash>
name: $splitIn
desc: Cuts a piece of text into separate parts you can pick out one by one.
category: String Utilities

<block type="text" title="How does the command work?">
text: $splitIn breaks a piece of text wherever the delimiter you give it appears and keeps the pieces for the rest of the command. Nothing is printed on its own, the parts simply wait until you ask for one of them with $splitOut. From there you can take the first piece, the last piece, or any numbered part in between and use it wherever you would use any other value.
</block>

<block type="list" title="How to use it in the app">
item: Open the Commands tab
item: Tap + to start a new command
item: Give it a name in the Name field
item: Set the Prefix field to !welcome
item: Write your script in the editor
item: Paste the line below wherever you need it
item: Tap Save
</block>

<block type="code" title="Syntax">
text: $splitIn[text; delimiter]
</block>

<block type="code" title="Ready-to-copy Example">
text: $splitIn[apple,banana,cherry; ,]
text: $splitOut[1]
</block>

<block type="callout" kind="note">
text: Parts are counted from 1: give $splitOut a number, or < for the very first piece and > for the very last. Spaces around each piece are trimmed off as the text is cut, and an empty delimiter is refused.
</block>
</dash>

<details>
syntax: $splitIn[text; delimiter]
param: text|text|Required|The text to cut into pieces
param: delimiter|text|Required|What to cut between the pieces, for example a comma. It cannot be left empty.
</details>
