<dash>
name: $footerIcon
desc: Puts a small round picture beside the footer of a card you are building.
category: Embed Builder

<block type="text" title="How does the command work?">
text: $footerIcon attaches a picture to the bottom line of the card you are building, the little circle that sits next to the footer text. Hand it any piece of text containing a web address and the bot picks the first link it finds, so you can leave punctuation around it. If several cards are going out together, finish with the card number so the picture lands on the one you meant.
</block>

<block type="list" title="How to use it in the app">
item: Open the Commands tab
item: Tap + to start a new command
item: Give it a name in the Name field
item: Set the Prefix field to !welcome
item: Write your script in the editor
item: Paste the line below wherever you need it
item: Tap Save
</block>

<block type="code" title="Syntax">
text: $footerIcon[url;(index)]
</block>

<block type="code" title="Ready-to-copy Example">
text: $footer[(c) My Community]
text: $footerIcon[https://example.com/logo.png]
</block>

<block type="callout" kind="note">
text: The address has to start with http:// or https://. Anything stuck on the end of it, a full stop or a closing bracket for instance, is trimmed away for you, and text with no valid link in it at all is refused.
</block>
</dash>

<details>
syntax: $footerIcon[url;(index)]
param: url|text|Required|Text holding the picture address, beginning with http:// or https://
param: index|number|Optional|Which card to put the picture on when you are building several at once (default 1)
</details>
