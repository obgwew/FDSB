<dash>
name: $sort
desc: Puts a list of numbers in order and hands back all of them or just the top few.
category: Math & Numbers

<block type="text" title="How does the command work?">
text: Give $sort a run of numbers, then tell it the direction, how many to return and what to put between them. asc orders from smallest to largest and desc does the opposite, and a return amount of -1 keeps the whole list.
text: Decimals and negative numbers are accepted, and the values come back as they were written, not rewritten. The result can be dropped into a sentence or posted on a line of its own.
</block>

<block type="list" title="How to use it in the app">
item: Open the Commands tab
item: Tap + to start a new command
item: Give it a name in the Name field
item: Set the Prefix field to $sort
item: Write your script in the editor
item: Paste the line below wherever you need it
item: Tap Save
</block>

<block type="code" title="Syntax">
text: $sort[number;number;...;asc/desc;returnAmount;separator]
</block>

<block type="code" title="Ready-to-copy Example">
text: $sort[42;7;19;9;66;desc;3;, ]
</block>

<block type="callout" kind="note">
text: A return amount of -1 means every number, while any other amount must be 1 or more. If there are fewer numbers than the amount, you simply get them all back.
</block>

<block type="callout" kind="warning">
text: Every value before the direction has to be a real number. One word or an empty slot stops the command with an error naming the value it could not read.
</block>
</dash>

<details>
syntax: $sort[number;number;...;asc/desc;returnAmount;separator]
param: number|number|Required|The numbers to sort; give one or more before the direction
param: asc/desc|text|Required|asc from smallest to largest, desc from largest to smallest
param: returnAmount|text|Required|How many values to return, or -1 for all of them
param: separator|text|Required|What goes between the values, such as a comma, or a name: dot, com, apo, sem, colon
</details>
