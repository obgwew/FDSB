<dash>
name: $sendEmbedMessage
desc: Posts a coloured card to a channel with a title, text, colour and footer.
category: Messaging

<block type="text" title="How does the command work?">
text: $sendEmbedMessage builds a card out of the parts you give it and posts it in the channel you name. The parts come in a fixed order, channel, title, description, colour, then footer, and members see the title on top, the body in the middle and the small footer line at the bottom. After the footer you can keep going with more parts, an author, an author icon, a footer icon, a thumbnail, a picture, a yes to add the time, and a last yes that appends the new message ID.
</block>

<block type="list" title="How to use it in the app">
item: Open the Commands tab
item: Tap + to start a new command
item: Give it a name in the Name field
item: Set the Prefix field to !embed
item: Paste the code below into the editor
item: Tap Save
</block>

<block type="code" title="Syntax">
text: $sendEmbedMessage[channelID; title; description; color; footer]
</block>

<block type="code" title="Ready-to-copy Example">
text: $sendEmbedMessage[$channelID; Welcome!; Thanks for joining; #00FF00; Enjoy your stay]
</block>

<block type="callout" kind="note">
text: The colour part takes a hex value such as #FF5733 and paints the stripe down the left side of the card. Anything you leave out is simply kept off the card, and only the channel is really needed to get one out.
</block>

<block type="callout" kind="warning">
text: The bot needs Send Messages and Embed Links in that channel, and it has to be able to see the channel at all. A channel it cannot reach gives an error rather than a card posted somewhere else.
</block>
</dash>

<details>
syntax: $sendEmbedMessage[channelID; title; description; color; footer]
param: channelID|snowflake|Required|Which channel the card is posted in
param: title|text|Optional|The heading at the top of the card
param: description|text|Optional|The body text of the card
param: color|text|Optional|The stripe colour, as a hex value such as #FF5733
param: footer|text|Optional|The small line at the bottom of the card
</details>
