<dash>
name: $removeSplitOutElement
desc: Takes one entry out of a list you split apart earlier.
category: String Utilities

<block type="text" title="How does the command work?">
text: Once $splitIn has broken a line of text into separate pieces, this drops one of them out of the list. Everything you keep stays in the order it already had, so removing the second of three leaves you holding the first and the third. Send the trimmed list back out with $joinSplitOut once you are done.
</block>

<block type="list" title="How to use it in the app">
item: Open the Commands tab
item: Tap + to start a new command
item: Give it a name in the Name field
item: Set the Prefix field to !clean
item: Write your script in the editor
item: Paste the line below wherever you need it
item: Tap Save
</block>

<block type="code" title="Syntax">
text: $removeSplitOutElement[index]
</block>

<block type="code" title="Ready-to-copy Example">
text: $splitIn[apple,banana,cherry; ,]
text: $removeSplitOutElement[2]
text: $joinSplitOut[; ]
</block>

<block type="callout" kind="note">
text: Counting starts at 1, not 0. A position that is not a whole number, or one that falls outside the list you split, stops the command with an error rather than quietly doing nothing.
</block>
</dash>

<details>
syntax: $removeSplitOutElement[index]
param: index|number|Required|Which piece to drop, counting from 1
</details>
