<dash>
name: $mod
desc: Hands back what is left over after dividing one number by another.
category: Math & Numbers

<block type="text" title="How does the command work?">
text: Give it two or more numbers and the bot divides the first by the second, then reports what is left over. Add a third number and it is applied to the answer in turn, so several divisions can be chained in one go. $mod[10; 3] comes back as 1, which is the remainder of 10 divided by 3.
</block>

<block type="list" title="How to use it in the app">
item: Open the Commands tab
item: Tap + to start a new command
item: Give it a name in the Name field
item: Set the Prefix field to $mod
item: Write your script in the editor
item: Paste the line below wherever you need it
item: Tap Save
</block>

<block type="code" title="Syntax">
text: $mod[a; b; ...]
</block>

<block type="code" title="Ready-to-copy Example">
text: $sendMessage[$mod[10; 3]]
</block>

<block type="callout" kind="warning">
text: Dividing by zero stops the command with an error, and anything that is not a number in the brackets is refused the same way.
</block>
</dash>

<details>
syntax: $mod[a; b; ...]
param: a|number|Required|The number being divided
param: b|number|Required|The number to divide by, never 0. Any extra numbers are applied one after another
</details>
