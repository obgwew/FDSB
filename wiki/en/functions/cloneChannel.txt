<dash>
name: $cloneChannel
desc: Copies an existing channel, settings and all, into a second one.
category: Channel Management

<block type="text" title="How does the command work?">
text: Point it at a channel and the bot makes a duplicate of it, carrying across its topic, its settings and where it sits in the server. Text, voice, category, stage and forum channels can all be copied. Add a second part and the copy gets that name instead of the original one, and the bot then posts a mention of the new channel so it is one click away in the chat.
</block>

<block type="list" title="How to use it in the app">
item: Open the Commands tab
item: Tap + to start a new command
item: Give it a name in the Name field
item: Set the Prefix field to !clone
item: Paste the code below into the editor
item: Tap Save
</block>

<block type="code" title="Syntax">
text: $cloneChannel[channel ID or channel mention; (new name)]
</block>

<block type="code" title="Ready-to-copy Example">
text: $cloneChannel[<#123456789012345678>; Archive]
</block>

<block type="callout" kind="warning">
text: The bot needs the Manage Channels permission and the command only works inside a server. Mention the channel you want copied — a plain name on its own will not do.
</block>

<block type="callout" kind="note">
text: Give it a line of its own instead of slotting it into the middle of a sentence: it copies the channel as a side effect and hands back nothing to print.
</block>
</dash>

<details>
syntax: $cloneChannel[channel ID or channel mention; (new name)]
param: channelID|text|Required|Which channel to copy, by its mention or its ID
param: newName|text|Optional|What to call the copy; leave empty to reuse the original name
</details>
