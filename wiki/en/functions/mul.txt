<dash>
name: $mul
desc: Multiplies the numbers you give it and hands the product back.
category: Math & Numbers

<block type="text" title="How does the command work?">
text: Hand it two or more numbers separated by semicolons and the bot works out their product. Two numbers multiplied together, or a whole column of them, give one answer back, so $mul[3; 4] comes out as 12 and $mul[2; 3; 4] as 24. It fits neatly inside a message, a title or a card body, which means a running total or a price can be written in a single line.
</block>

<block type="list" title="How to use it in the app">
item: Open the Commands tab
item: Tap + to start a new command
item: Give it a name in the Name field
item: Set the Prefix field to !calc
item: Write your script in the editor
item: Paste the line below wherever you need it
item: Tap Save
</block>

<block type="code" title="Syntax">
text: $mul[a; b; ...]
</block>

<block type="code" title="Ready-to-copy Example">
text: $sendMessage[$mul[3; 4]]
</block>

<block type="callout" kind="note">
text: You need at least two numbers and every part has to be a real number, otherwise the command stops with an error. An answer landing on a whole number comes back without a decimal point, and anything else keeps its decimals.
</block>

<block type="callout" kind="important">
text: It has to sit inside another command rather than on a line of its own, the way the example shows. Standalone there is nowhere for the answer to go, so the bot reports it as an unknown command.
</block>
</dash>

<details>
syntax: $mul[a; b; ...]
param: a|number|Required|The first number to multiply
param: b|number|Required|The second number to multiply. Add as many more as you like.
</details>
