<dash>
name: $color
desc: Paints the coloured strip down the side of your embed card.
category: Embed Builder

<block type="text" title="How does the command work?">
text: The card your command is building takes its colour from this line, shown as the strip down the left edge once the card is sent at the end. Use a hex value such as #FF5733 or one of the colour names and that is the shade you get. Anything the bot cannot make sense of quietly falls back to the usual dark grey bar.
</block>

<block type="list" title="How to use it in the app">
item: Open the Commands tab
item: Tap + to start a new command
item: Give it a name in the Name field
item: Set the Prefix field to !welcome
item: Write your script in the editor
item: Paste the line below wherever you need it
item: Tap Save
</block>

<block type="code" title="Syntax">
text: $color[hex or name]
</block>

<block type="code" title="Ready-to-copy Example">
text: $color[#FF5733]
</block>

<block type="callout" kind="note">
text: Leave it out and the card keeps the default dark strip. Building several cards at once? Add the card number after the colour to paint them one by one.
</block>
</dash>

<details>
syntax: $color[hex or name]
param: hex or name|text|Required|A hex value like #FF5733, or a colour name
</details>
