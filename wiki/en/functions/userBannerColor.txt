<dash>
name: $userBannerColor
desc: Gives you the accent colour behind a member's profile banner as a hex value you can use.
category: Info

<block type="text" title="How does the command work?">
text: Put a user ID or a mention in the brackets and the bot reports the accent colour sitting behind that person's banner; leave them out and you get the bot's own. What comes back is the #RRGGBB shape that colour fields and embed bars already expect, in capital letters. It is a quick way to tint a card to match whoever you are talking about.
</block>

<block type="list" title="How to use it in the app">
item: Open the Commands tab
item: Tap + to start a new command
item: Give it a name in the Name field
item: Set the Prefix field to !themecolor
item: Write your script in the editor
item: Paste the line below wherever you need it
item: Tap Save
</block>

<block type="code" title="Syntax">
text: $userBannerColor[(user_id)]
</block>

<block type="code" title="Ready-to-copy Example">
text: $userBannerColor[123456789012345678]
</block>

<block type="callout" kind="warning">
text: Only members who picked an accent colour have one to give, and the bot still has to track them down first. When nothing comes back the standalone form says so in the channel rather than posting an empty line.
</block>

<block type="callout" kind="note">
text: Used inside another line it works only from members the bot already knows about, and a colour of all zeroes counts as nothing worth showing. With no argument it always means the bot's own colour, never the member who ran the command.
</block>
</dash>

<details>
syntax: $userBannerColor[(user_id)]
param: userID|snowflake|Optional|The member to look up, as an ID or a mention; leave it out and you get the bot's own accent colour
</details>
