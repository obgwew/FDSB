<dash>
name: $div
desc: Divides the first number by each of the others, working left to right.
category: Math & Numbers

<block type="text" title="How does the command work?">
text: Give it two numbers and it divides the first by the second. Add a third or a fourth and the running result is divided again, in the order you wrote them. On its own it prints the answer; inside a line it is filled in wherever you typed it.
</block>

<block type="list" title="How to use it in the app">
item: Open the Commands tab
item: Tap + to start a new command
item: Give it a name in the Name field
item: Set the Prefix field to !welcome
item: Write your script in the editor
item: Paste the line below wherever you need it
item: Tap Save
</block>

<block type="code" title="Syntax">
text: $div[a; b; ...]
</block>

<block type="code" title="Ready-to-copy Example">
text: $sendMessage[$div[10; 2]]
</block>

<block type="callout" kind="warning">
text: Dividing by zero stops your command with an error, and so does asking for fewer than two numbers. Text where a number belongs is refused the same way.
</block>

<block type="callout" kind="note">
text: Answers that land on a whole number come back without decimals, so 10 divided by 2 is 5 and not 5.0. Everything is applied left to right: 10 divided by 2 and then by 5 gives you 1.
</block>
</dash>

<details>
syntax: $div[a; b; ...]
param: a|number|Required|The number you start dividing from
param: b|number|Required|What to divide it by; zero is refused. Extra numbers keep dividing the running total.
</details>
