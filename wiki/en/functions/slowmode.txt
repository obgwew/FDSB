<dash>
name: $slowmode
desc: Sets how often members are allowed to post in a channel.
category: Moderation

<block type="text" title="How does the command work?">
text: $slowmode puts a gap between messages in the channel you name. Give it a channel and a length such as 10s and members then have to wait that long before posting again, which takes the heat out of a busy channel without locking it. Use 0s to lift the limit and let everyone type as often as they like, the same way you found it.
</block>

<block type="list" title="How to use it in the app">
item: Open the Commands tab
item: Tap + to start a new command
item: Give it a name in the Name field
item: Set the Prefix field to !slowmode
item: Paste the code below into the editor
item: Tap Save
</block>

<block type="code" title="Syntax">
text: $slowmode[channelID; time]
</block>

<block type="code" title="Ready-to-copy Example">
text: $slowmode[$channelID; 10s]
</block>

<block type="callout" kind="note">
text: A length is a number plus a unit: s for seconds, m for minutes, h for hours. Six hours is the ceiling, so anything longer is turned down, and 0s switches slowmode off again in one go.
</block>

<block type="callout" kind="warning">
text: The bot needs Manage Channels in that channel before the change takes. A channel type that has no slowmode setting at all, a category for instance, is refused with an error instead of being changed.
</block>
</dash>

<details>
syntax: $slowmode[channelID; time]
param: channelID|snowflake|Required|Which channel to put the gap in
param: time|text|Required|How long the gap lasts, as a number plus s, m or h. 0s switches slowmode off.
</details>
