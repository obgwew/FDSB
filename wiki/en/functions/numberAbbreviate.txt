<dash>
name: $numberAbbreviate
desc: Shortens a large whole number into a compact form with a letter on the end.
category: Math & Numbers

<block type="text" title="How does the command work?">
text: Hand it a whole number and it comes back trimmed down to something readable, with a letter tacked on: 1500 turns into 1.5k, 1000000 into 1m and 2500000 into 2.5m. Anything below a thousand is handed back untouched, and a leading minus sign survives the trip. A good fit for member counts and other totals that would otherwise fill the whole screen.
</block>

<block type="list" title="How to use it in the app">
item: Open the Commands tab
item: Tap + to start a new command
item: Give it a name in the Name field
item: Set the Prefix field to !views
item: Write your script in the editor
item: Paste the line below wherever you need it
item: Tap Save
</block>

<block type="code" title="Syntax">
text: $numberAbbreviate[number]
</block>

<block type="code" title="Ready-to-copy Example">
text: $numberAbbreviate[1523487]
</block>

<block type="callout" kind="note">
text: The letters run k, m, b, t, qa, qi, sx, sp, oc, no and dc. Digits too many for even the last one are printed in full rather than shortened any further.
</block>

<block type="callout" kind="warning">
text: Whole numbers only. A decimal point or any stray letter and the bot posts an error instead of shortening part of it.
</block>
</dash>

<details>
syntax: $numberAbbreviate[number]
param: number|number|Required|The whole number to shorten; a leading - is kept
</details>
