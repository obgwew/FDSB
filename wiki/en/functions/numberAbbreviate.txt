<dash>
name: $numberAbbreviate
desc: Converts a whole number into a short abbreviated form with a suffix (k, m, b, ...).
category: Math & Numbers

<block type="text" title="How does the command work?">
text: $numberAbbreviate takes a whole number and rounds it to its short form using a suffix: k (thousand), m (million), b (billion), t (trillion), and so on. For example 1500 becomes 1.5k and 2500000 becomes 2.5m. Negative numbers are handled as well.
</block>

<block type="code" title="Syntax">
text: $numberAbbreviate[number]
</block>

<block type="code" title="Ready-to-copy Example">
text: $numberAbbreviate[1523487]
</block>

<block type="callout" kind="note">
text: Supported suffixes: k, m, b, t, qa, qi, sx, sp, oc, no, dc. Numbers smaller than 1000 are returned unchanged.
</block>

<block type="callout" kind="warning">
text: Only whole numbers are accepted. Decimals and non-numeric characters return an error.
</block>
</dash>

<details>
syntax: $numberAbbreviate[number]
param: number|number|Required|A whole number to abbreviate (may include a leading - sign)
</details>
