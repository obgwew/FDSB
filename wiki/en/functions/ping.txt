<dash>
name: $ping
desc: Gives you how quickly the bot is talking to Discord, in milliseconds.
category: Utilities

<block type="text" title="How does the command work?">
text: $ping measures the round trip between the bot and Discord and hands back the figure in milliseconds. Drop it into a line of text and the bot posts that number in the channel, so members can judge the connection for themselves. A small number means the bot is reacting quickly, a large one means something is slowing the link down right now.
</block>

<block type="list" title="How to use it in the app">
item: Open the Commands tab
item: Tap + to start a new command
item: Give it a name in the Name field
item: Set the Prefix field to !speed
item: Write your script in the editor
item: Paste the line below wherever you need it
item: Tap Save
</block>

<block type="code" title="Syntax">
text: $ping
</block>

<block type="code" title="Ready-to-copy Example">
text: $ping pong!
</block>

<block type="callout" kind="note">
text: The lower the figure, the snappier the bot feels in the channel. It measures the connection between the bot and Discord rather than anything on your device, so the same value can change from one minute to the next.
</block>
</dash>
