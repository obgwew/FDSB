<dash>
name: $dm
desc: Sends everything your command says straight into someone's private messages.
category: Messaging

<block type="text" title="How does the command work?">
text: Stand this line above your messages and everything they send afterwards leaves the channel and arrives in a private conversation instead. Used with no brackets the recipient is whoever typed the command; give it a member's ID or mention and it is that person instead. The switch lasts only until the end of this run, so it never leaks into your next command.
</block>

<block type="list" title="How to use it in the app">
item: Open the Commands tab
item: Tap + to start a new command
item: Give it a name in the Name field
item: Set the Prefix field to !notify
item: Paste the code below into the editor
item: Tap Save
</block>

<block type="code" title="Syntax">
text: $dm
text: $dm[(userID/mention)]
</block>

<block type="code" title="Ready-to-copy Example">
text: $dm
text: $sendMessage[Check your DMs!]
</block>

<block type="callout" kind="warning">
text: The two of you have to share a server, and the recipient needs private messages switched on. Otherwise the bot cannot deliver anything, and the person waiting in the channel never finds out why nothing arrived.
</block>

<block type="callout" kind="note">
text: Keep it above the messages that should go private. Anything you send before this line stays in the public channel where everyone can read it.
</block>
</dash>

<details>
syntax: $dm[(userID/mention)]
param: userID/mention|text|Optional|Who should receive the private messages; leave empty for whoever typed the command
</details>
