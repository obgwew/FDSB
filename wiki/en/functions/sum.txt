<dash>
name: $sum
desc: Adds up every number you hand it and hands back the total.
category: Math & Numbers

<block type="text" title="How does the command work?">
text: Put two or more numbers in and the bot adds them all together, however many you feel like listing. You get a clean figure back, with the decimal point dropped whenever the total lands on a whole number. Because it settles to a value rather than a message, you can drop it straight inside another command's brackets and let the total appear mid-sentence.
</block>

<block type="list" title="How to use it in the app">
item: Open the Commands tab
item: Tap + to start a new command
item: Give it a name in the Name field
item: Set the Prefix field to !cart
item: Write your script in the editor
item: Paste the line below wherever you need it
item: Tap Save
</block>

<block type="code" title="Syntax">
text: $sum[a; b; ...]
</block>

<block type="code" title="Ready-to-copy Example">
text: $sendMessage[$sum[8; 2]]
</block>

<block type="callout" kind="note">
text: A good companion to $sub, $mul and $div when you are working through a basket total or a set of scores. On its own line the total is posted by itself; nested inside $sendMessage it lands in the middle of your own wording.
</block>

<block type="callout" kind="warning">
text: Two numbers at the very least are needed, and a stray letter anywhere in the list stops the whole sum with an error. Leave a space in the middle of the list and it is read as zero rather than upsetting the calculation.
</block>
</dash>

<details>
syntax: $sum[a; b; ...]
param: a|number|Required|The first number to add up
param: b|number|Required|The second number to add up, and you can keep going with as many more as you like
</details>
