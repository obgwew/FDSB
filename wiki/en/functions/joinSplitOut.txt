<dash>
name: $joinSplitOut
desc: Glues the pieces produced by $splitIn back together with a separator between them.
category: String Utilities

<block type="text" title="How does the command work?">
text: $joinSplitOut takes everything $splitIn broke apart and puts it back into one piece of text, with a separator of your choosing in between.
text: Leave the separator out and a single space is used instead, so the quickest form is just $joinSplitOut with nothing after it.
text: The finished line joins whatever else the bot is about to say, which makes it easy to build a sentence out of split parts.
</block>

<block type="list" title="How to use it in the app">
item: Open the Commands tab
item: Tap + to start a new command
item: Give it a name in the Name field
item: Set the Prefix field to !join
item: Write your script in the editor
item: Paste the line below wherever you need it
item: Tap Save
</block>

<block type="code" title="Syntax">
text: $joinSplitOut[separator]
</block>

<block type="code" title="Ready-to-copy Example">
text: $splitIn[apple,banana,cherry; ,]
text: $joinSplitOut[ + ]
</block>

<block type="callout" kind="note">
text: $splitIn has to run first, otherwise there is nothing to join. Give $joinSplitOut an empty separator and the pieces are joined with one space each.
</block>
</dash>

<details>
syntax: $joinSplitOut[separator]
param: separator|text|Required|The text placed between every two pieces
</details>
