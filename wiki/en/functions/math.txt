<dash>
name: $math
desc: Works out a calculation and posts the answer in the channel.
category: Math & Numbers

<block type="text" title="How does the command work?">
text: Type a calculation between the plus, minus, multiply, divide and power signs and the bot sends back the result. Rounding helpers and the usual trigonometry are in there too, along with the constants pi, e and inf. Only arithmetic is accepted, so nothing else can ride along inside your expression.
</block>

<block type="list" title="How to use it in the app">
item: Open the Commands tab
item: Tap + to start a new command
item: Give it a name in the Name field
item: Set the Prefix field to $math
item: Write your script in the editor
item: Paste the line below wherever you need it
item: Tap Save
</block>

<block type="code" title="Syntax">
text: $math[expression]
</block>

<block type="code" title="Ready-to-copy Example">
text: $math[2 + 2 * 3]
</block>

<block type="options" title="Supported operators">
option: +|Adds the two numbers
option: -|Takes the second number off the first
option: *|Multiplies
option: /|Divides
option: //|Divides and drops the decimal part
option: %|What is left over after dividing
option: **|Raises to a power
</block>

<block type="options" title="Supported functions">
option: sqrt|Square root
option: abs|How far the number is from zero
option: ceil|Rounds up to the next whole number
option: floor|Rounds down to the whole number below
option: round|Rounds to the nearest whole number
option: log/log2/log10|Logarithm in base 10, 2 or e
option: sin/cos/tan|Sine, cosine and tangent
</block>

<block type="callout" kind="note">
text: Dividing by zero comes back as an error rather than a number, and a whole result drops the decimal point — 8 comes back as 8, not 8.0.
</block>
</dash>

<details>
syntax: $math[expression]
param: expression|text|Required|The calculation to work out, such as 2 + 2, sqrt(16) or pi * 2
</details>
