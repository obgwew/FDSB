<dash>
name: $endfunc
desc: Closes an FDScript function block.
category: Flow Control

<block type="text" title="How does the command work?">
text: $endfunc closes the most recently opened $func block. It marks the end of a reusable function definition and must appear in a matching pair with $func.
</block>

<block type="code" title="Syntax">
text: $endfunc
</block>

<block type="code" title="Ready-to-copy Example">
text: $func[ping]
$sendMessage[Pong]
$endfunc
</block>

<block type="callout" kind="warning">
text: An $endfunc without a matching $func, or with a different open block in between, is a script error.
</block>
</dash>

<details>
syntax: $endfunc
</details>
