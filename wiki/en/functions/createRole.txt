<dash>
name: $createRole
desc: Creates a new role in the server with the name, colour and permissions you choose.
category: Moderation

<block type="text" title="How does the command work?">
text: $createRole adds a new role to the server where the command was used.
text: You give it a name, a colour, and how much power the role should carry, either one of the presets below or a raw permission number.
text: You can also decide whether the role is listed on its own next to members and whether anyone is allowed to ping it, and both of those stay off unless you say yes.
text: The finished role appears in your server's role list straight away, and its ID can be picked up with $return[createdRoleID] to hand to a later command.
</block>

<block type="list" title="How to use it in the app">
item: Open the Commands tab
item: Tap + to start a new command
item: Give it a name in the Name field
item: Set the Prefix field to !role
item: Paste the code below into the editor
item: Tap Save
</block>

<block type="code" title="Syntax">
text: $createRole[role name; color; permissions; (display role; mention role)]
</block>

<block type="code" title="Ready-to-copy Example">
text: $createRole[VIP; #FFD700; member; yes; no]
</block>

<block type="options" title="Permission presets">
option: cosmetic|No permissions at all, just a coloured name
option: member|Normal member powers: send messages, react, upload files, join voice
option: mod / moderator|Kicking, banning, timeouts and message management
option: manager / admin|Everything a moderator can do, plus channels, roles, webhooks and emojis
</block>

<block type="callout" kind="note">
text: The bot needs the Manage Roles permission in that server, and the command only works inside a server, never in a private chat.
text: Discord's own limit of 100 characters applies to the role name.
</block>
</dash>

<details>
syntax: $createRole[role name; color; permissions; (display role; mention role)]
param: role name|text|Required|The name shown for the new role, up to 100 characters
param: color|text|Required|A hex colour such as #FF5733, or empty for the default
param: permissions|text|Required|One of the presets above, or the raw permission number
param: display role|yes/no|Optional|yes to list the role on its own beside members (default: no)
param: mention role|yes/no|Optional|yes to let everybody ping the role (default: no)
</details>
