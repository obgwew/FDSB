<dash>
name: $footer
desc: Adds a line of small grey text along the bottom of an embed card.
category: Embed Builder

<block type="text" title="How does the command work?">
text: Whatever you hand it becomes the footer line of the card you are building, sitting underneath everything else in smaller, lighter lettering. That is the natural home for a credit, a date, or a short remark about what the card is showing. Because it only shapes the card, nothing appears until you send the embed.
</block>

<block type="list" title="How to use it in the app">
item: Open the Commands tab
item: Tap + to start a new command
item: Give it a name in the Name field
item: Set the Prefix field to !card
item: Write your script in the editor
item: Paste the line below wherever you need it
item: Tap Save
</block>

<block type="code" title="Syntax">
text: $footer[text]
</block>

<block type="code" title="Ready-to-copy Example">
text: $footer[Requested by $authorName]
</block>

<block type="callout" kind="note">
text: Add $footerIcon when you want a tiny picture beside that line. Calling $footer a second time simply overwrites the words you set the first time round.
</block>
</dash>

<details>
syntax: $footer[text]
param: text|text|Required|The words to print along the card footer
</details>
