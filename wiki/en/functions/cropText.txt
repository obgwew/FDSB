<dash>
name: $cropText
desc: Shortens a piece of text to a length you choose and marks the cut.
category: String Utilities

<block type="text" title="How does the command work?">
text: Pass it some text and a maximum length, and what comes back is never longer than that. If the original was over the limit it is cut right there and whatever ending you supplied is added on, three dots being the usual choice. Text that already fits is handed back exactly as it was, ending and all. It is the standard way to stop a long quote from overflowing a narrow field.
</block>

<block type="list" title="How to use it in the app">
item: Open the Commands tab
item: Tap + to start a new command
item: Give it a name in the Name field
item: Set the Prefix field to $cropText
item: Write your script in the editor
item: Paste the line below wherever you need it
item: Tap Save
</block>

<block type="code" title="Syntax">
text: $cropText[text; max characters; (ending)]
</block>

<block type="code" title="Ready-to-copy Example">
text: $cropText[This is a very long message; 10; ...]
</block>

<block type="callout" kind="note">
text: The length has to be a whole number of zero or more; anything else stops the command with an error. Leave the ending out and the text is simply chopped at the limit with no marker at all.
</block>
</dash>

<details>
syntax: $cropText[text; max characters; (ending)]
param: text|text|Required|The text to shorten
param: max characters|number|Required|How many characters to keep at most
param: ending|text|Optional|What to tack on after the cut, such as ...
</details>
