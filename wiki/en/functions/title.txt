<dash>
name: $title
desc: Puts the bold heading at the top of the card your command builds.
category: Embed Builder

<block type="text" title="How does the command work?">
text: Whatever you place between the brackets becomes the bold heading across the top of the card, sitting above the description and the coloured bar. Discord's own formatting works inside it, so bold, italics and links all come through. When one command builds more than one card, add the card's number after the text to fill in the second or third instead of the first.
</block>

<block type="list" title="How to use it in the app">
item: Open the Commands tab
item: Tap + to start a new command
item: Give it a name in the Name field
item: Set the Prefix field to !rules
item: Write your script in the editor
item: Paste the line below wherever you need it
item: Tap Save
</block>

<block type="code" title="Syntax">
text: $title[text]
</block>

<block type="code" title="Ready-to-copy Example">
text: $title[Server Rules]
</block>

<block type="callout" kind="note">
text: The heading only shows up once the card is actually sent, so a $title on its own line does nothing visible until something puts it on screen. Keep it to a single sensible line — a heading Discord turns down stops the whole card from being delivered.
</block>
</dash>

<details>
syntax: $title[text]
param: text|text|Required|The heading shown across the top of the card
</details>
