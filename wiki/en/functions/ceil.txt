<dash>
name: $ceil
desc: Rounds a number up to the next whole number.
category: Math & Numbers

<block type="text" title="How does the command work?">
text: Give it a single number and you get that number turned upwards, so 3.1 comes back as 4 and 3.9 gives 4 as well. A number that is already whole is handed back untouched, and rounding a negative value upwards moves it towards zero, putting -2.5 up to -2. It drops straight into a line of text wherever you need a figure with no decimal left on it.
</block>

<block type="list" title="How to use it in the app">
item: Open the Commands tab
item: Tap + to start a new command
item: Give it a name in the Name field
item: Set the Prefix field to $ceil
item: Write your script in the editor
item: Paste the line below wherever you need it
item: Tap Save
</block>

<block type="code" title="Syntax">
text: $ceil[number]
</block>

<block type="code" title="Ready-to-copy Example">
text: $sendMessage[Result is $ceil[3.2]]
</block>

<block type="callout" kind="note">
text: One number goes in and one whole number comes out: 3.1 gives 4, -2.5 gives -2, and 5.0 stays 5. Anything that is not a number at all is turned away with an error.
</block>
</dash>

<details>
syntax: $ceil[number]
param: number|number|Required|The number to round upwards, such as 3.1, -2.5 or 10
</details>
