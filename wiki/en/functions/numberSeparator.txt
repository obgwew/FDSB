<dash>
name: $numberSeparator
desc: Formats a whole number with a thousands separator for easier reading.
category: Math & Numbers

<block type="text" title="How does the command work?">
text: $numberSeparator takes a whole number and inserts a separator every three digits from the right (thousands grouping). By default the separator is a comma (,), and you can pass a custom separator as the second argument. For example 1234567 becomes 1,234,567.
</block>

<block type="code" title="Syntax">
text: $numberSeparator[number]
text: $numberSeparator[number; separator]
</block>

<block type="code" title="Ready-to-copy Example">
text: $numberSeparator[1234567]
</block>

<block type="code" title="Ready-to-copy Example (custom separator)">
text: $numberSeparator[1234567; .]
</block>

<block type="callout" kind="note">
text: Negative numbers keep their - sign: for example -1234567 becomes -1,234,567.
</block>

<block type="callout" kind="warning">
text: Only whole numbers are accepted. Decimals and non-numeric characters return an error.
</block>
</dash>

<details>
syntax: $numberSeparator[number; (separator)]
param: number|number|Required|A whole number to format (may include a leading - sign)
param: separator|text|Optional|The separator to insert every three digits (default: ,)
</details>
