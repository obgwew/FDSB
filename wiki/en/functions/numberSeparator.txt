<dash>
name: $numberSeparator
desc: Groups a long whole number into readable blocks of three digits.
category: Math & Numbers

<block type="text" title="How does the command work?">
text: Put a number in and the bot breaks its digits into threes counting from the right, so 1234567 reads as 1,234,567. A comma does the separating unless you hand it a second part holding your own, such as a dot or a space. A minus sign on a negative number stays out in front of the first group.
</block>

<block type="list" title="How to use it in the app">
item: Open the Commands tab
item: Tap + to start a new command
item: Give it a name in the Name field
item: Set the Prefix field to !stats
item: Write your script in the editor
item: Paste the line below wherever you need it
item: Tap Save
</block>

<block type="code" title="Syntax">
text: $numberSeparator[number]
text: $numberSeparator[number; separator]
</block>

<block type="code" title="Ready-to-copy Example">
text: $numberSeparator[1234567]
</block>

<block type="code" title="Ready-to-copy Example (custom separator)">
text: $numberSeparator[1234567; .]
</block>

<block type="callout" kind="note">
text: A three-digit number such as 500 comes back exactly as typed, since there is only one group to place a separator between. Blank out the second part and the comma is used again.
</block>

<block type="callout" kind="warning">
text: A decimal point or any letter makes the bot post an error rather than formatting only the digits it recognises.
</block>
</dash>

<details>
syntax: $numberSeparator[number; (separator)]
param: number|number|Required|The whole number to group up; a leading - is kept
param: separator|text|Optional|What to place between the groups; a comma when left empty
</details>
