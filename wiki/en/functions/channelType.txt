<dash>
name: $channelType
desc: Says what kind of channel something is: text, voice, category, forum or stage.
category: Channel

<block type="text" title="How does the command work?">
text: Point it at a channel and you get that channel's kind back as a single word. Leave the brackets empty and it reports on the channel the command was used in instead. The answer comes in handy for guarding actions that only make sense in one kind of place.
</block>

<block type="list" title="How to use it in the app">
item: Open the Commands tab
item: Tap + to start a new command
item: Give it a name in the Name field
item: Set the Prefix field to $channelType
item: Write your script in the editor
item: Paste the line below wherever you need it
item: Tap Save
</block>

<block type="code" title="Syntax">
text: $channelType[channel mention/ID]
</block>

<block type="code" title="Ready-to-copy Example">
text: $channelType[<#123456789012345678>]
</block>

<block type="callout" kind="note">
text: A channel the bot cannot find, or brackets holding something that is not a channel ID, come back as unknown rather than an error. Check for that word before you act on the result.
</block>
</dash>

<details>
syntax: $channelType[channel mention/ID]
param: channel|text|Optional|Which channel to look at, by mention or ID. Leave empty for the current one.
</details>
