Privacy Policy

FDSB – Free Design Studio Bot (package: com.freedesingstudiobot.FDSB)
Last updated: October 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the FDSB app (“the App”), developed by obgwew (“the Developer”, “we”), handles information. FDSB lets you create and run Discord bots locally on your own device.

1. Summary

The App does not have its own servers or user accounts. We do not collect, receive, sell, or share your personal information. Everything you create or enter in the App stays on your device.

2. Information stored on your device

To work, the App stores the following only on your device:

  • Discord bot tokens that you enter, stored using the device’s secure storage.
  • Scripts, commands, and settings that you create inside the App.
  • Logs and app preferences needed for the App to function.

This data is never sent to the Developer. You can delete it at any time (see section 7).

3. Network access and third parties

The App uses the internet to connect directly from your device to Discord’s services using the bot token you provide. This connection is between your device and Discord; the Developer is not involved in it and does not see it.

Discord processes data under its own terms and privacy policy: https://discord.com/privacy.

Bots that you write and run may read or send Discord data (for example, server messages or member information) depending on the scripts you create. You are responsible for how your bots use that data and for complying with Discord’s policies and applicable laws. That data is handled on your device and is not transmitted to the Developer.

4. Permissions used by the App

  • Internet / network state: to connect to Discord and detect connectivity.
  • Notifications: to show the status of your running bot.
  • Foreground service (data sync) and wake lock: to keep your bot running while the screen is off.
  • Ignore battery optimizations: so the system does not stop your bot in the background.

These permissions are used only for the features described above.

5. Advertising, analytics, and tracking

The App does not include advertising, analytics, or tracking services, and it does not use your data for advertising or profiling.

6. Data sharing and security

We do not share your data with anyone, because we do not receive it. Bot tokens are kept in the device’s secure storage. Keep your tokens private and regenerate a token in the Discord Developer Portal if you believe it has been exposed.

7. Retention and deletion

Data stays on your device until you remove it. You can delete bots and settings inside the App, clear the App’s storage in Android settings, or uninstall the App to remove all of its local data. Because the Developer holds no copy of your data, there is nothing to request from us.

8. Children’s privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and Discord itself requires users to meet its minimum age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

9. Open source

The App is open source. You can review its code at https://github.com/obgwew/FDSB.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page, with the “Last updated” date changed accordingly.

11. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact: obgwew@gmail.com or open an issue at github.com/obgwew/FDSB/issues.
